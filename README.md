# Ning Yang | Academic Homepage

This repository hosts the personal academic website of Ning Yang, a Ph.D. candidate in Civil Engineering at the Institute of Rock and Soil Mechanics, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

His research focuses on earthquake physics, fault friction and healing, fluid-induced seismicity, thermo-hydro-mechanical coupling, and their applications in deep Earth and energy engineering.

Visit the website: [https://younnin.github.io/](https://younnin.github.io/)

## Website Content

The website presents selected research interests, peer-reviewed publications, Chinese invention patents, education, conference presentations, academic exchanges, engineering projects, honors, and contact information.
